# Review Rubric — Wireless and Monitoring

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Repeatable locations/time/client/tool version | 15 |
| Coverage and capacity treated separately | 15 |
| RSSI, noise/SNR, utilization, retry, and local throughput interpreted correctly | 20 |
| Internet/WAN separated from WLAN/LAN test | 15 |
| One controlled change with before/after evidence | 15 |
| Alerts include duration, owner, escalation, and recovery | 10 |
| Privacy, authorization, rollback, and documentation | 10 |

A strong conclusion might find strong RSSI but high utilization/retry during the meeting peak, then validate narrower channels, coordinated channel reuse, power/cell review, or additional capacity. It does not prescribe more APs without a channel/capacity plan.
