# N10-009 V9 Acronym Guide — Objective Document 6.0

Know the expansion and practical meaning, not only the letters. The wording below is concise and independent; use the linked chapters for full explanations and scenarios.

## How to study the list

1. Cover the middle column and expand each acronym aloud.
2. Cover the last column and state one use, one risk, or one comparison.
3. Group confusing terms: `RPO/RTO`, `IDS/IPS`, `SFP/QSFP`, `SSID/BSSID/ESSID`, `DoH/DoT`, and `STP/RSTP`.
4. Do not infer security from a name. For example, GRE and PPTP are not modern encryption choices, and a captive portal is not Wi-Fi encryption.
